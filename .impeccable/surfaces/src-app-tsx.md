## Scope

The whole site: one page, mode Operate (the visitor's success is a checked manuscript and a list of
corrections). Students and MDC reviewers use it the same way.

## Audience, job, constraints

Drop a manuscript (.docx, .doc, .pdf, .odt, .rtf); read it in the browser; see all 38 checklist
items with statuses; fix what is listed using page numbers and quotes; download the comments as
.docx or .pdf. Never say "upload". Unofficial, alpha, AI-built disclaimer in header, notice strip,
results, footer, FAQ and report. Template and checklist downloads.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A guided desk in three stages, Prepare, Check, Correct, that walks one manuscript through
the MDC checklist part by part; it refuses the category default of a lone upload box over a wall of
mixed results.

OWN-WORLD: The UPOU OER system: paper ground, charcoal ink, UP maroon action fills, forest for
passed, gold for in-progress, Instrument Serif for stage titles and big numerals, Instrument Sans for
everything operable, 18px cards on 1px lines. Signature move: the UP tri-colour stripe is the step
rail; it fills maroon, then gold, then forest as the manuscript moves from Prepare to Check to
Correct, and each manuscript part in the overview fills forest when all its items pass.

STORY: The visitor learns the file stays on their device, drops it, watches the nine parts get
checked, then walks the parts, fixing what is listed at the pages named, and downloads the comments.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Header (wordmark, Unofficial · Alpha badge, theme switch), notice strip, the
three-stage rail with the current stage lit. Stage 1: serif headline and privacy line on the left,
the drop zone with its Choose a file button on the right (stacked on phones), template and
checklist downloads beneath.

FORM: Guided three steps, position 7 of 7 on the ordered list, dealt lead; seed key f9f677dc.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
